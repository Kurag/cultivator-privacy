Privacy Policy for Cultivator Life

Effective date: 2026-11-10
Developer: Kurag (individual developer)
Contact: kurag101@gmail.com
App: Cultivator Life (Android package com.kurag.cultivator)

Cultivator Life is a single-player, real-world map game. This policy explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics and no game server of its own (testers can optionally send feedback recordings to the developer, see below). Your location and step count never leave your phone. If you sign in to Google Play Games, Google stores a backup of your game progress and your leaderboard scores in your Play Games account; we, the developer, do not receive a copy.

1. Information the app uses

Location

If you grant permission, the app uses your device's precise or approximate location, only while the app is open on screen, to show you on the map and to decide which herbs and other things appear nearby. This happens on your device. Your location is not sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer, and is not stored outside your device.

Physical activity (step count)

If you grant the Physical activity permission, the app reads your phone's step counter to convert your steps into in-game qi, including steps taken while the app was closed (credited when you next open it). This is processed on your device only. Step data is not uploaded or shared. You can use the app without this permission, but walking will not generate qi from steps.

Save data

Your game progress (cultivation level, items, herbs, beasts, totals such as steps and distance walked) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved to your Play Games account (see below). To make exploring rewarding, the save also records which map squares (about 200 m across) you have visited and which herbs you have already gathered, plus the place you chose as your in-game "cave abode", if you set one. That is a coarse record of where you have played, so the cloud copy lives only in your own Play Games account.

Feedback recordings (testers only, optional)

Testers can choose to send the developer a feedback report with a three-finger double-tap or Settings > Tester tools > Record feedback. Nothing is recorded unless you start it, and a "Stop and send" button shows the whole time. A report contains a recording of the game screen (a few frames per second, up to 90 seconds), your voice from the microphone if you allow the Microphone permission (otherwise an optional typed note), and game details: build number, cultivation level, companion, which panel is open, whether GPS has a fix, and the tail of the app's log. It does not include your coordinates. Reports go over HTTPS to the developer's own feedback server and are used only to find and fix problems. When the developer closes a report, the video and voice recording are deleted; a few still images, the transcript and the game details are kept as the record of the fix. You can ask for your reports to be deleted at the contact address below.

2. Information sent to third parties

Google Play Games Services

The app signs you in to Google Play Games automatically if you have a Play Games profile on your phone. You can play without it. When you are signed in:

  • The app receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google, to show who is signed in. They stay on your phone.
  • Your game progress is saved to Google's Saved Games service in your Google account (stored in hidden app data in your Google Drive), so you can restore it after reinstalling or on a new phone. The developer cannot read it.
  • Your leaderboard scores (distance walked, weekly steps, herbs gathered, codex entries) and achievements are sent to Google Play Games. Who can see them depends on your Play Games profile visibility settings.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the Play Games terms. You can turn off or delete your Play Games profile and game data in the Google Play Games settings.

Map tiles from OpenFreeMap

To draw the map, the app downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Each request includes the tile coordinates for the area you are viewing and, as with any web request, your IP address and basic technical details such as your device's user agent. This means the tile server can tell roughly which area of the world is being viewed and from which IP address. We do not control or receive this data. See OpenFreeMap (https://openfreemap.org) for how they handle requests.

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and © OpenMapTiles.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google, as the distributor, may process information about your installation and device under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your personal information through Google Play, beyond aggregate statistics and crash reports that Google makes available to developers.

3. What we do not do

  • No accounts of our own: the only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games one.
  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not upload your location or steps anywhere. Your save backup goes only to your own Play Games account.
  • No in-app purchases in this version.

4. Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: clear the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Cultivator Life > Storage, or uninstall the app.
  • In Play Games: your cloud save, scores and achievements are kept in your Google account until you delete them. Open the Google Play Games app (or play.google.com/games), go to Settings, and delete your data for Cultivator Life, or delete your Play Games profile.
  • Apart from tester feedback reports (see above), we hold no server-side copy of your data. You can revoke the Location or Physical activity permission at any time in Android Settings.

5. Children

Cultivator Life is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app is intended for ages 13 and up.

6. Security

Map tiles and Play Games data are sent over HTTPS. Save data on your phone is stored in the app's private storage; protecting your device (screen lock, updates) protects that data.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and its effective date before that change is released.
